## 1. Executive summary

MobiDuka is a stable MVP-stage digital retail operations platform designed for Kenyan micro, small and medium-sized businesses (MSMEs), including dukas, kiosks, mini-shops, pharmacies, agrovets and growing multi-branch retailers. Its current features are available for review through the product demo. It brings point-of-sale (POS), inventory, expense and cash-session records, customer accounts, staff access controls and business reporting into one system.

The product combines a web application with mobile point-of-sale workflows. Selected checkout and expense workflows can continue when a device is temporarily offline: supported records are saved locally and queued for synchronization when connectivity returns. This is intended to help merchants maintain continuity during network interruptions; not every feature or transaction type is available offline.

The MVP includes supported M-Pesa point-of-sale flows through Safaricom Daraja. The sandbox integration has passed testing; production access is pending to validate a direct Till workflow with a real Till. MobiDuka is a software platform; it does not itself hold or settle merchant funds.

## 3. The retail challenge

Many independent retailers manage sales, stock, expenses, customer credit and staff activity using paper records or disconnected tools. This can make it difficult to maintain accurate records, understand business performance and keep checkout operations moving when connectivity is unreliable. Owners and managers may also need visibility into activity across staff members, devices or branches.

MobiDuka is designed to bring these everyday retail workflows together in a practical system that can be used by business owners, managers, accountants and cashiers.

## 4. Product and capabilities

MobiDuka provides a shared digital workspace for day-to-day retail operations, including:

- **Point of sale:** Product lookup, checkout, payment-method recording and receipts.
- **Inventory management:** Product catalogs, stock tracking, low-stock visibility, suppliers and purchase orders.
- **Cash and expenses:** Cash-session workflows, expense records and relevant sales reporting.
- **Customer accounts:** Customer profiles and credit-ledger workflows for merchants that offer customer credit.
- **Staff access:** Business-scoped accounts with role-based permissions for owners, supervisors, cashiers and accountants.
- **Reporting:** Sales, expenses, inventory and cash-session summaries to support operational review.
- **Barcode scanning and receipts:** Camera-based product barcode lookup and support for compatible Bluetooth thermal printers.
- **Selected offline workflows:** The mobile application can save supported cash sales and new cash expenses locally for later synchronization. Other workflows may require an internet connection.

## 5. Customers and market

MobiDuka is designed for:

- Independent dukas, kiosks and corner shops.
- Mini-markets and convenience retailers.
- Pharmacies and agrovets.
- Retail businesses adding staff, tills or branches.

The initial market focus is Kenya. Expansion into other markets may be considered as product, regulatory and commercial readiness allow. MobiDuka is currently pre-pilot, with no verified active merchant users yet. Prospective merchants have expressed interest in direct Till payment workflows, which require production Daraja access and credentials before those workflows can be validated with a live Till.

## 6. Safaricom and M-Pesa integration

The MVP includes support for selected M-Pesa payment workflows through Safaricom Daraja APIs, including STK Push initiation, transaction status queries and payment callback handling. The Daraja sandbox integration has passed testing. Production access is not yet available; access is needed to validate the direct Till workflow with a real merchant Till. The intended setup uses each merchant’s own Till and Daraja credentials, subject to the applicable Safaricom requirements. MobiDuka is seeking guidance on the process and requirements for obtaining production access.

The integration is configured for each business using that business’s M-Pesa account details and Daraja credentials. Credentials are stored in encrypted form and scoped to the relevant business account. Merchants remain responsible for their own M-Pesa account, settlement arrangements, fees and compliance obligations.

The availability of production services depends on production access, merchant account configuration and applicable Daraja requirements. MobiDuka does not represent that it is a Safaricom product, agent, or approved payment service provider.

## 8. Value proposition

MobiDuka is designed to help merchants:

- Bring routine sales, stock, expense and customer-account records into one system.
- Give staff access appropriate to their operational roles.
- Review business activity through sales and operating reports.
- Continue supported cash-sale and expense workflows during temporary connectivity interruptions, then synchronize queued records when online.
- Use M-Pesa payment requests within supported checkout workflows.

These are intended product benefits and should not be presented as measured customer outcomes unless supported by verified pilot or customer data.
